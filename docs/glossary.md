# Glossary

**Agent** — AI-enabled interface using instructions, knowledge and tools to support a defined task.

**Data contract** — explicit schema, cadence, semantics and quality rules for a data product.

**Grip gap** — a risk/issue where the operational next step is incomplete, such as no action, owner, deadline, mitigation or closure.

**Narrative evidence** — approved emails, chats, notes, documents or transcripts describing what was said/agreed.

**Operational snapshot** — point-in-time structured data extract.

**Provenance** — information that allows a claim to be traced back to its source and timestamp.

**Signal vs noise** — distinguishing meaningful sustained movement from ordinary fluctuation.

**Staleness threshold** — the maximum acceptable age of a source before a warning is required.
